About NEW GEN EV

We make EV charging smart, simple and honest.

NEW GEN EV is a smart RFID charging station built end to end by our student team — the hardware, the firmware and this live dashboard. Tap your card, plug in, and watch every volt, amp and rupee update in real time.

Who we serve

One station. Three kinds of people.

From the driver plugging in to the person who owns the charger, everyone sees the same live, honest numbers.

EV drivers

Charge without the paperwork. Your card is your key, and your phone shows the bill as it grows.

  • Tap RFID card to start
  • Live voltage, current and bill
  • Book a slot before you arrive

Station owners

Run a charger like a pro from one dashboard — sessions, bookings, customers and location in one place.

  • Every session logged with units and bill
  • Booking alerts and customer list
  • Share a link so people book your station

Campuses & communities

Colleges, housing societies and offices can host a charger anywhere — the map follows the station wherever it goes.

  • Live location on the dashboard
  • Fair, first-come slot system
  • Emergency stop and safety alerts
Why we exist
Our mission

Make every charge visible and fair.

Real meter readings, live on your phone, billed at a clear per-unit price. If you can see it, you can trust it.

Our vision

A smart charger in every college, society and street.

Affordable stations that anyone can host, book and monitor — built in India, for the way India moves.

A note from our team
“

We started NEW GEN EV with a simple question: why does charging an electric vehicle still feel like a guessing game? You plug in, wait, and only find out what you used at the end.

So we built a station that shows everything while it happens — the live reading from a real energy meter, the running bill, and a dashboard you can open from anywhere. Every part of it, from the wiring to the website, was built by our team.

This is just the beginning. Thank you for charging with us.
