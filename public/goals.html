Our Mission

To make EV charging simple, transparent and accessible — a smart, RFID-based station that any EV owner can book, use and pay for in real time, backed by data every step of the way.

What we're working toward
⚡

Reliable, real-time charging

Live voltage, current, power and energy readings straight from the meter — no guesswork, no waiting to find out what happened during a session.

📅

Zero-hassle booking

Reserve a slot in advance, and if you don't show up within 15 minutes, it's released automatically — fair access for everyone, no manual coordination needed.

🔒

Safety first

Emergency-stop monitoring, relay protection, and an always-on dashboard mean the station can be watched and controlled from anywhere.

🌱

Supporting cleaner mobility

Every session tracked here is a step toward making EV adoption easier — reliable infrastructure is one of the biggest barriers to going electric, and that's what we're building.

📊

Transparent billing

Bills are calculated directly from real energy consumed (not estimates), so every user sees exactly what they used and what they owe.

🚀

Built to grow

From a single station today to a network tomorrow — the same dashboard, booking system and app are designed to scale to more chargers and more locations.
