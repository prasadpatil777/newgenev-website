Customers open /pay.html (or scan the QR on the charger screen), pay your UPI for an exact amount, and charging starts once the payment is confirmed. It stops by itself when the paid amount is used.

How it works
  • 1A customer plugs in, but their RFID card doesn't work.
  • 2You tap Start charging twice. The command reaches the charger with its next reading (about half a second).
  • 3The charger starts a normal session: meter, bill and timer count as usual. Stop it here, with any RFID card, or with the emergency button.
No internet at the charger? Connect your phone to the NEW_GEN_EV Wi-Fi, open 192.168.4.1 and use the Remote control box there.
