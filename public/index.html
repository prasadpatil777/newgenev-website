How it works

From tap to full charge, three steps

01

Tap your card

Hold your registered RFID card to the reader at the station. The relay switches on within a second.

02

Watch it charge

Open your dashboard to see live voltage, current, power and a running timer for the session.

03

Pay what you used

When you unplug, the session closes and the bill is calculated from the actual units drawn — nothing more.
